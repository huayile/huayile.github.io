Research

My research is in 3D computer vision. I am interested in how machines can understand the dynamic 3D world from everyday videos and, in the long run, how robots can use this understanding to act in it. Currently, I work on 4D reconstruction of dynamic scenes from a single casual video.

DynMF2: Rigid Motion Factorization by Expectation-Maximization for Monocular 4D Reconstruction and Motion Segmentation
Agelos Kratimenos*, Yile Hua*, Kostas Daniilidis
arXiv, 2026
arXiv

We discover the few rigid motions behind a dynamic scene from a single video, using Expectation-Maximization on 3D point trajectories and no masks. Each 3D Gaussian follows exactly one motion, so the motions that reconstruct the scene in 4D also segment what moves.

* Equal contribution.
